FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum ad spend to work with you?

We typically work with brands spending at least $5k/mo on ads, so we have enough data to optimize effectively. If you're below that, we can help you build toward it.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. All plans are month-to-month. We earn your business every month through results, not contracts.

Which industries do you specialize in?

We specialize in DTC e-commerce brands across beauty, apparel, home goods, and health & wellness — but our process adapts to any product-based business.

How often will I get reporting?

Depending on your plan, you'll get weekly or bi-weekly performance reports plus real-time access to a live reporting dashboard.

How fast can we get started?

After your strategy call, most onboarding is completed within 3-5 business days, with campaigns launching the following week.
